These are the only voices used for character speech. The user chooses each character's voice; never choose for them. Offer three or four that fit the character, from this list, and let them listen to Flow's preview first.

## How a voice gets onto a character

In Flow, a character's page has "Select a voice". Clicking a voice applies it at once and closes the dialog; searching only highlights it. The Flow runner extension does this when it creates characters (guide: doc `flow-guide`). A voice preview can speak any sentence up to 120 characters: that is how Pryce's refused EP02 line was recorded.
