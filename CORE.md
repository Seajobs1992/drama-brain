# Core rules (every task, no exceptions)

1. Authority for anything visible: the user's latest correction, then the scene master and the location authority, then the neighbouring shot, then identity sheets, then prop references. Text only fills gaps.
2. The user picks faces, casting and voices from options. Never choose or invent them.
3. Spend credits only when the user asked for that work. One take of each item. Retake only if it fails a check or the user asks.
4. Dialogue is the script's exact words. Never reword, add or drop a line.
5. Sound: Google Flow voices only, no ElevenLabs. Effects and ambience yes. Music never inside a clip.
6. Never mirror, flip or fake a fix. Regenerate or edit properly, and disclose every local edit.
7. Nothing is done until it is checked against its reference, picture and words. Say plainly what was not verified. Your selection is not the user's approval.
8. Before saying a rule or document does not exist, search: `brain.py find "<words>"`. Usually the rule exists and was not applied.
9. When the user corrects you: fix it, then record it: `brain.py lesson "<what went wrong, and the rule>" --card <name>`.
10. Privacy: never send the user's name, email or account names to outside services.
11. Writing: plain English, short sentences, no em dashes.
12. Read the card for your task. Open a full document only at the section the card names: `brain.py section <doc> "<heading>"`.
