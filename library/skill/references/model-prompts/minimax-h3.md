# MiniMax H3 prompt guide (Pollo `minimax` / `minimax-h3`)

Use only for MiniMax H3. Nothing here applies to Wan, Flow or Seedance.

**Role on The Man They Erased:** a lip reference take. When Wan's lips fail twice on the same frame, make a MiniMax H3 take of the line from the same keyframe; the user approves its lips; Wan then copies it (see wan-3.0.md, "If the lips fail twice"). Proven: Episode 1 clip 3c ("This works"), Episode 3 C09 and C12 ("Both are good").

**Mode:** image-to-video. `image` = the approved keyframe, the true first frame. It makes sound even though there is no sound switch. Durations 4 to 15 s; 768p costs 20 credits for 4 s, 40 for 8 s (verify live). Output is 1344x768: scale in the edit.

**Prompt (short, one paragraph):**

    Medium close-up, slow cinematic push-in. The man in the dark headband stands behind his workbench in the shed, three-quarter view, looking at the man off-screen left, and says in English, steady and tired: "I got Seo-yeon out. Then I went back in." Natural English lip sync, clear male voice, a brief pause between the two sentences. He never looks into the lens. No music, no subtitles.

Shape: shot size and camera move; the person by clothing, place, eye target; "says in English, <manner>:" and the exact line; "Natural English lip sync, clear <male/female> voice"; "never looks into the lens"; "No music, no subtitles." Keep the shot plan's camera move; never lock the camera unless the plan says so.

**Watch for:** MiniMax may wait before speaking and squeeze the line into the end (E3 C12 started at 2.6 s of 4.4 s). If the user wants it earlier, ask for the line "from the first second".
