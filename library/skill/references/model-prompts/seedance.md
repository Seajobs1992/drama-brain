# Seedance prompt guide (Pollo)

Use only for Seedance. Not in current use on The Man They Erased (the user chose Wan); never suggest it unasked. Source: invideo's model guide plus our 23 Nov 2026 test on clip 3c.

- Length: past about 150 words Seedance drops instructions. Keep each prompt as short as the beats allow; identity, prop state and look go in the references and a short lock line.
- Dialogue in double quotes is the lip-sync signal. Keep each line under about 12 words; split a longer locked line across shots without changing a word. Say what is heard in the last seconds, or a long clip trails into silence.
- Time brackets `[00:00 - 00:03]` make hard cuts; write "one continuous shot, no cuts" when none is wanted.
- Write camera motion apart from subject motion ("she turns; the camera holds"). One camera move per shot.
- Each reference has one job and one named token; attachment order is binding.
- End with a `Preserve:` line naming the identity, the prop state and the composition to hold.
- Identity references: front plus left and right three-quarter views, face 30 to 50 percent of the frame. Re-attach them every generation.
- Two resolved faces per shot; crowds as soft background.
- Between takes, change one thing at a time.
- "No drift" means nothing changes that the plan did not ask for; keep the plan's camera move (a locked camera on 3c looked flat and cost 96 credits). Seedance lip syncs without a still camera.
