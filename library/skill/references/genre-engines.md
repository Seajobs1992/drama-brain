# Genre engines

Choose the section that fits the brief. A hybrid should have a clear primary payoff and a secondary source of pressure. Do not mechanically combine every popular trope.

## Revenge and family betrayal

Engine: a concrete injustice creates a goal; the protagonist gathers leverage and chooses an arena where it changes power. Escalation can move from private denial to public cost, but every confrontation needs new evidence, a changed alliance or a more expensive choice.

Keep a clue chain: origin, access, possession, what it proves and who can challenge it. A confession is different from an allegation, an arrest from a conviction, and restored wealth from restored trust. Close the promised estate, death or reputation thread even if romance also resolves.

Observed contrast: MG-004 times evidence for public reversal; MG-003 changes interpretation through documents and delayed explanations. Transfer the evidence timing, not their specific family plots. Avoid convenient cameras, unexplained rescues and repeated accusations after decisive proof.

## Romance, hidden children and chosen family

Engine: attraction or belonging conflicts with a concrete reason not to trust. Show care through choices before relying on status or gifts. Keep a knowledge chart when parentage or identity is hidden; each disclosure must change a decision, not just increase the number of people who know.

Give children distinct objectives, voices, faces and actions. Adults should demonstrate care before learning the child is useful, magical or biologically theirs. Keep protection, access and custody promises observable.

For a redemption romance, write the harmful pattern, a specific admission, a costly behavioral test and the other person’s freely chosen response. Sacrifice may contribute to change; it does not erase prior coercion. Respect a requested dark or morally complicated tone without presenting pressure as freely given consent.

Observed contrast: MG-012’s partial paternity reveals produce suspense, but unsafe access undermines luxury gifts. Its late respected refusal is a clearer change. MG-001 builds chosen family through practical care. MG-011 distinguishes rescue from forgiveness. A family reunion and an independent departure can both be valid endings.

## Professional competence and concealed identity

Engine: viewers recognize capability while an institution or rival misreads it. Give the expert a meaningful constraint, such as responsibility, access, evidence or a consequence of exposure. Do not make every colleague foolish merely to display genius.

Show an observable problem, the lead’s relevant observation, a decision and a consequence. Let colleagues earn ally status through actions. Use a recurring professional object or procedure whose meaning changes as responsibility changes.

MG-008 turns a signature from danger into solidarity; MG-007 uses a respectful witness before a wealth reveal. These are dramatic patterns, not validated clinical or legal protocols. If the original scene depends on technical realism, research the specific claim through appropriate authoritative sources. Otherwise keep invented mechanisms clearly fictional and internally consistent.

## Progression and academy fantasy

Engine: desire meets a rule-governed obstacle; growth changes what can be attempted; success creates a cost or a new problem. Track the current ability, limit, price, injury, resource and established counter. A hidden strength is satisfying when earlier information makes its revelation intelligible.

Use repeated tests to measure meaningful change, not to repeat humiliation. Give allies abilities the lead cannot simply replace. If an authority changes rules after a win, make that injustice consequential rather than an unlimited reset device.

MG-005’s prepared handicap removal differs from MG-009’s household and artifact comedy. MG-010 makes an upgrade create danger and tests loyalty through a tempting illusion. Preserve those differences. Avoid unprepared resurrection, arbitrary rank inflation or a mentor test that endangers everyone without aftermath.

## Animal intermediaries and magical children

Engine: a private channel or gift reveals a need others misunderstand; useful care creates reciprocal trust. Give each animal or ally a distinct contribution and a reason to help. Prepare its useful ability before the climax.

Maintain a clear distinction between what the audience hears and what human characters hear. The protagonist needs a plausible public response when acting on private animal information. Do not let an animal voice disclose facts it could not know.

MG-006 uses private animal speech for comedy and practical solutions. MG-011 uses earlier care to prepare later rescue tasks. Avoid making love contingent on healing powers, using the same false accusation repeatedly, or allowing villains access to weapons through unexplained security failures.

## Friendship betrayal and emancipation

Engine: unequal obligations become visible; the protagonist refuses; the refusal exposes dependence and creates a practical or social cost. Track who does the work, pays, bears risk and controls the explanation.

Repurpose an established skill for self-protection. Make a boundary actionable rather than a speech alone. Test promises with the situation they claim to change. Grief can coexist with a maintained decision.

MG-013 turns record-keeping into a means of challenging identity misuse, belongings into evidence of unpaid work, and a shared photo archive into a new interpretation. Its final driver’s seat answers the opening exclusion. Avoid requiring a new romantic rescuer, equating illness with dishonesty, or stretching a single conclusion across repeated speeches.

## Choosing an ending

Ask which emotional promise was primary. Justice needs an accountability outcome; belonging needs freely offered care; mastery needs a meaningful application; escape needs actual separation or safety; independence needs control over a decision. A wedding cannot substitute for all of them.

An ending may intentionally leave a larger world open. Mark that choice honestly in the series map. Do not accidentally abandon a title promise or the foundational injustice because a late villain supplied an easier climax.
